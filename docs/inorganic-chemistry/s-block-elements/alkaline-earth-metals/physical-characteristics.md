# [![ChemistryEdu Logo](../../../images/favicon.svg){ align=left, width=3.8% }](../../../index.md)  Alkaline Earth Metals | Physical Characteristics

In this article, we will discuss the physical properties of alkaline earth metals like appearance, density, melting and boiling points, electropositive character and characteristic flame test.

## Appearance and Hardness

* The alkaline earth metals are, in general, silvery white, lustrous and relatively soft.
* However, they are **harder** than the alkali metals because they have two valence electrons, which gives stronger metallic bonding.
* Beryllium and magnesium appear to be somewhat greyish.

## Density of Alkaline Earth Metals

* Alkaline earth metals are denser than the corresponding alkali metals because of their smaller size and greater atomic mass.
* Density first decreases from Be to Ca and then increases up to Ra.

| Element   | Density / g cm<sup>-3</sup>  |
|-----------|------------------------------|
| Be        | 1.84                         |
| Mg        | 1.74                         |
| Ca        | 1.55                         |
| Sr        | 2.63                         |
| Ba        | 3.59                         |
| Ra        | (5.5)                        |

## Melting and Boiling Points of Alkaline Earth Metals

* The melting and boiling points of alkaline earth metals are **higher** than those of the corresponding alkali metals. This is due to their smaller size and two valence electrons, which make metallic bonding stronger.
* The trend down the group is, however, **not systematic**. Beryllium has the highest melting point and magnesium has the lowest.

| Element   | m.p. / K  | b.p. / K  |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Be        | 1560      | 2745      |
| Mg        | 924       | 1363      |
| Ca        | 1124      | 1767      |
| Sr        | 1062      | 1655      |
| Ba        | 1002      | 2078      |
| Ra        | 973       | (1973)    |

## Electropositive Character

* Because of their low ionization enthalpies, alkaline earth metals are strongly electropositive in nature.
* Electropositive character **increases down the group** from Be to Ba as the ionization enthalpy decreases.

!!! tip ""

    $$Be < Mg < Ca < Sr < Ba$$

## Characteristic flame test of Alkaline Earth Metals

* Calcium, strontium and barium impart characteristic colours to the flame.
* In the flame, the valence electrons are excited to higher energy levels. When they drop back to the ground state, energy is emitted in the form of visible light.

| Metal     | Characteristic Colour to flame  |
|-----------|---------------------------------|
| Ca        | Brick red                       |
| Sr        | Crimson                         |
| Ba        | Apple green                     |

* The electrons in beryllium and magnesium are too strongly bound (because of their small size and high ionization enthalpy) to get excited by the energy of the flame. Hence, **Be and Mg do not impart any colour to the flame**.
* The flame test for Ca, Sr and Ba is useful for their detection in qualitative analysis and for their estimation by flame photometry.

## Conductivity

* Like alkali metals, the alkaline earth metals have high electrical and thermal conductivities, which are typical characteristics of metals.
